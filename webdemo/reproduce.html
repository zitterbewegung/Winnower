Find rules worth running — search a rule family

This screens a sample of a rule family for rules that are still doing something at the horizon — each at its own best initial density — then runs the real selector on some of the survivors and ranks them by defect rate.

Committed comparison rows exist for all 256 ECA rules (seeds 11–15, T ∈ {50, 100, 200, 400, 800}), ECA 30/54/110 (seeds 11–20, plus T = 600), and Diamoeba (seeds 11–15). Custom rules and other configurations run in exploration mode. The candidate search grid is the paper's default for each dimension.
